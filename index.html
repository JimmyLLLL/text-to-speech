Free Text to Speech Converter

Convert any text into natural-sounding speech with our free online TTS tool. Choose from multiple languages and voices, adjust the speaking rate and pitch, and listen instantly. No signup, no download — everything runs in your browser using the Web Speech API.

How It Works

The tool uses your browser's built-in Speech Synthesis engine (Web Speech API). When you enter text and click Speak, the browser converts the text into spoken audio in real-time. Available voices depend on your operating system and browser — most modern browsers include voices for major languages.

Features

  • Multiple languages — English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Japanese, and more
  • Multiple voices — Select from all voices available on your device
  • Adjustable speed — Slow down (0.5x) or speed up (2x) the speech
  • Adjustable pitch — Lower or raise the voice pitch
  • Play, pause, resume, stop — Full playback control
  • Sample texts — Quick-start with built-in examples
  • 100% private — All processing happens in your browser

Use Cases

  • Accessibility — Help visually impaired users read text content
  • Language learning — Hear correct pronunciation of words and phrases
  • Content creation — Generate voiceovers for videos and presentations
  • Proofreading — Listen to your writing to catch errors
  • E-learning — Add audio narration to educational materials
  • Reading long articles — Let the browser read while you multitask

Privacy

Your text is 100% private. All speech synthesis happens locally in your browser. No text is sent to any server.
